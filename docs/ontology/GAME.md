# Game

Draft, agreed in conversation on 2026-11-17. Exact numbers and rules go into [RULES.md](RULES.md), not here. Placeholders marked **TBD** are tracked in [OPEN_DECISIONS.md](OPEN_DECISIONS.md).

## Goal
The Player with the most Points at the end of the playing period wins. Territories are an important side feature on top of that. A tie is decided by [R-R01](RULES.md).

- **TBD, playing period:** how long it lasts and when it starts and ends ([D-06](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).

## Points
- Every alcoholic Drink earns Points, wherever it is drunk, and counts towards the Player's total account.
- Drink categories for now: beer, wine, shots, long drinks. Beer and wine also need an amount.
- Drinks only count from Thursday to Sunday.
- Nobody else has to confirm a Drink.

- **TBD, Points per Drink:** how many Points each category gives, and how the amount counts for beer and wine ([D-02](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).
- **TBD, Game Night boundaries:** when an evening starts and ends, and which evening a Drink after midnight belongs to ([D-05](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).

## Territories
- Territories are predefined areas on a map.
- The Player's location assigns a Drink to a Territory. Bars do not matter for this.
- Every Territory has its own account for each Player. A Drink inside a Territory earns the same Points on that Territory's account as on the total account.
- A Drink outside every Territory only counts towards the total account.

- **TBD, Territory areas:** how the areas are drawn and stored on the map ([D-03](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).

## Ownership
- The Player with the most Points on a Territory's account owns it, and the map shows this.
- Ownership lasts for the whole playing period, but any Player can take a Territory at any time by overtaking the Owner.
- Owning a Territory gives an advantage over other Players who drink there, so that Owners can earn disproportionately many Points.

- **TBD, owner advantage:** whether the Owner takes a small share of the Points other Players earn there, or weakens those Players' Points on that Territory ([D-01](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).

## Minigames
- Examples: virtual beer pong, a reaction test.
- Each Player may play one Minigame per evening.
- Minigames give no Points. They give special items (for example a Points multiplier).

- **TBD, special items:** which items exist and what each one does ([D-04](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).
- **TBD, interruption:** whether an interrupted Minigame counts as the Player's one play for that evening ([D-07](OPEN_DECISIONS.md)).
